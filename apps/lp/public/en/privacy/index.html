[operator name] ("we", "us") sets out below how we handle information about you in the smartphone app "Katarute" (the "Service").

1. Scope

This policy applies to information we obtain in providing the Service. Third-party services linked from the Service handle information under their own policies.

2. Information we collect

InformationDetails
Photos Images of problems and notes that you take or choose.
Conversation transcripts Your conversation with the Service, converted to text. We do not store the audio itself.
Device ID An anonymous ID the Service issues to your device, used instead of an account. Using the app never requires your name, email address or phone number (contact details you choose to give us when you contact support are covered in the next row).
Support requests What you send through the support form or by email. Only if you ask for a reply do we collect the email address you enter. If you include your device ID, we collect that too. Both are optional.
Learning records What your senpai wrote on the board, the review questions made from it, your answers and their correct/incorrect classification, your review status, and the days you used the Service.
Purchase status Whether a subscription is active. Payment itself is handled by the App Store / Google Play; we never receive your card number.
Diagnostics Records of app errors and performance.

3. Purposes

  1. Providing the Service (generating the lesson, creating review questions, grading answers, sending notifications)
  2. Understanding how the Service is used, and improving its quality and features (section 4)
  3. Investigating and fixing problems
  4. Preventing misuse
  5. Responding to support requests
  6. Complying with the law

4. Use for improving the Service

We may use the information in section 2, including photos and conversation transcripts, to improve the Service: finding explanations that are unclear or wrong, improving the questions we generate, and reproducing and fixing bugs.

  1. This applies on the free tier and on Premium alike.
  2. You can ask us to stop this use, or to delete your information, through the contact in section 10, quoting your device ID. After such a request we continue to use the information only to the extent needed to provide the Service itself (section 3, item 1).
  3. This use never links the information to your name or contact details.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not share your information with third parties without your consent, except:

  1. where required by law;
  2. where necessary to protect a person's life, body or property and consent is difficult to obtain;
  3. when the providers in section 6 process it on our behalf, to the extent needed for the purposes above.

7. Retention and deletion

Photos, conversation transcripts, learning records and purchase status are kept for as long as we provide the Service. We delete them, or render them unrecoverable, when you ask us to under section 10 or when the Service is discontinued. Diagnostics are deleted after roughly 90 days. You can ask us at any time to delete the information tied to your device by sending your device ID to the contact in section 10.

8. Advertising and tracking

The Service shows no ads. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we never sell or share your information for advertising.

9. Minors

The Service is intended for secondary-school students. It may not be used by anyone under 13. If you are under 18, use it with a parent's or guardian's consent. A paid subscription requires that consent.

10. Access, correction and deletion requests

You can ask us to disclose, correct, add to, delete or stop using your information. Because the Service has no accounts, please include the ID shown at Settings > Device ID (for support) in the app. Without it we may be unable to identify your information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy when necessary. Important changes will be announced in the Service or on this page.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy: kfukejob@gmail.com, or the form on the support page.
