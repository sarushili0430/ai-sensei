Closed beta

Join the closed beta

We hand out the build before the store listing exists. There are two things to do — join the Google Group, then open the Google Play opt-in link. Nothing is charged.

An Android device A Google account About 3 minutes

  1. 1Join the group

    Use the Google account that is signed in on your Android device. The group is the Google Play tester roster itself, so joining with a different account means step 2 tells you that you are not a tester and stops there.

    Join the group

    On the page that opens, press the “Join group” button next to the group name. Nothing counts until you press it.

    “You do not have permission” is not a mistake. The conversations are simply hidden from people who have not joined. Press “Join group” and you are in.

    If the button will not open, an empty email to ai-sensei+subscribe@googlegroups.com joins you just the same. The group address is ai-sensei@googlegroups.com.

  2. 2Open the Play opt-in link

    Once you are in the group, opt in on the Google Play side. That press is what puts the build on your device — being in the group alone does not deliver anything.

    Opens once the test build is out

    The opt-in URL is https://play.google.com/apps/testing/jp.co.aiSensei. Opening it today still says you are not a tester. When the build lands we post to the group and this button starts working. Finish step 1 and there is nothing else to do until then.

  3. 3Install from the Play Store

    Opting in unlocks the normal Play Store page for Kataru-te, and you install it like any other app. There is no account to create once the app opens.

    It is not always live immediately — anywhere from a few minutes to a few hours. If you get “item not found”, leave it a while and open the opt-in URL again.

If it does not work

It says I am not a tester

Almost always a mismatched account. Check that the account you joined the group with is the one your browser is signed in as — switching Google accounts has to happen before you open the opt-in URL, not after.

If the account is right, your membership has not reached Play yet. Give it a while and open the link again.

I can’t find it by searching the Play Store

You won’t. An app in closed testing never appears in search, so the opt-in URL is the only way in. After installing, it launches from your home screen like anything else.

Can I join on an iPhone?

Not yet. This runs on Google Play’s machinery, so the only builds it can hand out are Android ones. The iPhone build will go out through TestFlight, and that announcement goes to the same group — do step 1 now and the iOS invite reaches you too.

Does joining the group mean a lot of email?

No. Only we can post to it, and we post two things: that a build is out, and that something significant changed. It is not a place where testers talk to each other, which is also why your address stays invisible to the rest of them.

What taking part involves

Cost None. Premium features are open during the test and no checkout screen appears.
What we ask Roughly two weeks, opening it every other day for one problem. And a note whenever something breaks or an explanation looks wrong.
What we do not ask Star ratings, posts about it, or bringing friends in. None of that is a condition of taking part.
What becomes visible Joining shows your email address and display name to the developer — that is how Google Groups and the Play Console work. Other testers cannot see them; the group is configured that way. The app itself still runs on an anonymous device ID that is never tied to your address.
Leaving Leave the group and you are done. Nobody asks why and nobody tries to talk you out of it — deleting the app is enough on its own.
Your data The notebook photo and a transcript of the conversation are stored to build your karte, and to find explanations that were unclear or wrong. The details are in the privacy policy （日本語） and the terms （日本語）.
