Try this first

I denied the camera or microphone permission by mistake

You can allow it later in your device settings: on iPhone, Settings > Katarute; on Android, Settings > Apps > Katarute > Permissions. The camera is requested right before you take a photo and the microphone right before the conversation, so the rest of the app keeps working after a denial.

I took a photo but no topic shows up

Try again in good light with the whole problem in the frame. You can also pick a photo you already have. Only topics from the school stage you chose in Settings are offered (mathematics and English, at junior-high or high-school level in Japan, or the equivalent high-school courses elsewhere). Other subjects and levels cannot be read yet.

My senpai doesn't read the equations out loud

That is by design. Equations, calculations and figures go on the board; the voice only asks questions like "Look at D here". A formula read aloud does not stay in your head, so this is not a bug.

My voice isn't getting through / the conversation doesn't move on

Check the microphone permission and try a quieter place. If the words won't come, tap "I can't explain this yet" to move on — it is recorded, but never held against you. Tap "Got it" when you have it, or "That's it for today" to stop.

I can't start a new problem today

On the free plan you get one lesson from your senpai per day; the next one opens when the date changes. If you want several lessons a day, have a look at Premium.

I'm not getting the review notifications

Check that notifications are allowed. Settings > "Check-backs from your senpai" in the app opens the device notification settings. Reviews arrive 3 and 7 days after a lesson (and the next day after a wrong answer). We never send nagging notifications.

I bought Premium but nothing is unlocked

Try "Restore purchases" on the purchase screen. After changing phones or reinstalling, purchases come back as long as you use the same Apple ID / Google account you bought with. If that doesn't fix it, tell us through the form.

I think my senpai taught me something wrong

It can happen. The board stays on screen, so compare it with your textbook before tapping "Got it". The grading of the review question that arrives 3 days later can also be wrong. Report anything that felt off from Settings > "Report something that felt wrong", or through the form on this page.

Cancelling and refunds

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription. Cancellations and refunds are handled by the store you bought from. Deleting the app does not stop the subscription — cancel it in the store.

iPhone / iPad Settings > your name at the top > Subscriptions > Katarute > Cancel.
Refunds: ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
Android Google Play Store > your profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Katarute > Cancel.
Refunds: through Google Play Help.

We never handle the payment ourselves, so we cannot issue refunds directly. If you cancel mid-period, Premium stays on until the end of that period.

Deleting your data / stopping its use

You can ask us to delete, or stop using, your stored photos, transcripts, boards and review-question records (including their use for improving the service). There is no account, so please include the ID shown at Settings > Device ID (for support) in the app when you send the form. Without it we may be unable to identify your data.

What we collect and where it is stored is described in the privacy policy.

Requirements

  • iOS 15 or later / Android 7.0 or later
  • A camera (photos of the problem and your notes) and a microphone (talking with your senpai)
  • A stable internet connection — the conversation runs online
